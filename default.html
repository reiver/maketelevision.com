Era

I created an was writing for on this blog during the era when:

  • I was part of the videoblogging community. The community that:
    • were pioneers of video on the Internet and web
    • was organized on the old (and now gone) Yahoo! videoblogging mailing list
    • coined the words “vlog”, “vlogging”, etc
    • had the conference named: “vloggercon”
  • I was working on finding ways to make (what was then) the new world of video work on the Internet and Web work with HTTP, HTML, and the Web in general.
  • I was part of the Microformats community — that was trying to find more developer-friendly ways of making the information written into HTML files more machine-legible.
  • I was part of the WHATWG — that created HTML5. And:
    • I was trying to get an HTML <video> element to get added to HTML. It didn't exist before. But, I helped get it created.
